NSe Panel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 04/04/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RandomideaX collects, uses, stores, and shares data when you use NSe Panel.

Contact

- Developer: RandomideaX
- Contact email: randomideaxcontact@gmail.com

Data We Collect

NSe Panel may collect or process the following categories of data:

- We process your Account credentials that you enter for login and only saved on your local device, including username and password.
- Character and gameplay data returned by the connected game server, such as character name, level, XP, gold, tokens, server label, and action logs.
- Device and diagnostic data generated by Unity LevelPlay SDK and other third-party SDKs used in the app.
- Notification and background-service state required to keep automation running while the app is minimized.

How We Use Data

We use data to:

- Authenticate you with the selected game server.
- Let you save or reuse quick login information on your device.
- Run automation actions, refresh character state, and display logs.
- Grant and validate billing time used to unlock automation actions.
- Show ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance through Unity LevelPlay SDK.
- Maintain app security, detect issues, and improve reliability.

Data Sharing

We may share data with:

- Unity and mediated ad network partners, through Unity LevelPlay SDK, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Data Storage and Retention

- Quick login data is stored locally on the device using Android secure storage where available.
- Billing expiry time is stored locally on the device.

Security

We use HTTPS for network communication where supported and apply reasonable measures to protect stored and transmitted data. No system is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Advertising

NSe Panel uses Unity LevelPlay SDK to show rewarded ads for the +Time feature. Unity LevelPlay SDK and its mediated partners may collect data such as IP address, device identifiers, user product interactions, and diagnostic information.

Your Choices

- You can log out at any time.
- You can clear locally stored app data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage in Android settings.
- If you need help with privacy-related requests, contact the email listed above.

Children

NSe Panel is not directed to children under 13. The app is intended for general audiences who are old enough to use online account-based game tools and advertising-supported features. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will review the request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
